Amplified is a full-service creative studio born in Kampala. For over a decade we've helped startups, SMEs and household names turn up the volume on their stories.

Why we exist

To help our clients accelerate business growth while supporting underserved communities and faith-based organisations.

Founded in 2009 by university students as an audio recording studio, we've grown into a full-service creative agency and social enterprise — using design as a force for community impact.

By bringing strategy, design, film, sound and marketing under one roof, we give brands a single, coordinated voice — and then we make sure it's heard.

Our story

From a campus studio
to a creative agency.

We started as university students in 2009 with an audio recording studio producing music and audio commercials for small businesses in the community.

An early highlight was a campus concert that raised funds for underprivileged students. We also headlined a music competition that spotted and nurtured talent, offering free studio time to the winners with support from sponsors including Coca-Cola.

Over the years, we extended into digital marketing, design, film, photography, web and print, growing into the integrated creative studio we are today.

ASOMA

Amplified School of Major Acquaintance — structured youth development equipping young people with professional and life skills.

Amplified 4 Christ

A gospel outreach initiative using creative arts — music, design, and video — to spread hope and build faith-based communities.
